Farm Band

You are a farmer… and an orchestra conductor. Catch farm animals, house them in pens and conduct them as a band: every pen sings its solo, then the whole farm sings together as one orchestra.

Pillars

From the pitch slides
1 · CatchCatch new animals and bring them to your farm

Lasso animals in the meadow, on the beat. Rarer animals are harder to catch and sing better.

2 · ArrangeThey will be your band!

Pens are sections of the orchestra. Each pen has a style, a capacity and acoustics.

3 · ListenEach animal has its own properties and musical talents

Audition every animal. Stats change the real sound: off-key, off-beat, loud or soft.

4 · ConductCreate the best Moosic of the world!

Solos left to right, then the tutti. The grade pays coins, fans and new venues.

Farm = pens that hold the band. Music is always in key, so a band can only sound better, never wrong.

    Concerts & World

    Scoring · venues
    Drone › floorRhythm › timeMelody › tuneChorus › chords
    • Concert: each pen sings two bars alone, left to right, in storage order. Then all pens play the tutti together and end on one big chord.
    • Score: solos 70% (voice quality, affinity, pen fit) + orchestra 30% (registers, style variety, harmony between pens). Grades S to D.
    • Always in key: fixed chord progressions and pentatonic melodies. Low stats sound slightly off, never wrong.
    • Rewards: S 100 M · A 70 · B 45 · C 25 · D 10, plus fans. No fail state.
    • Venues: Barnyard, Village Square, County Fair, Concert Hall. Each crowd has a taste, such as the Fair loving Rhythm.
